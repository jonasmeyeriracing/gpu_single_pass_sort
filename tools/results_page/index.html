Draw-Call Sort Benchmarks

Each measurement is one batch of 20 independent sorts (0 to 8,192 elements each, 16-bit key with a 16-bit payload), dispatched without barriers and timed with GPU timestamps around the sort dispatches only. Values are medians over 1,000 batches on discrete GPUs and 300 on the integrated GPU, with the data evicted from the caches before every batch.
